# Antennas & Microwave Engineering Laboratory

This repository contains two laboratory exercises focused on the experimental characterization of antennas, microwave transmission lines, and waveguide structures as part of coursework in High Frequency Circiuits.

## Exercise A — Dipole Antenna Characterization

Experimental characterization of λ/2 dipole antennas using a **Spectrum Analyzer** and **Microwave Generator**.

Main measurements and analysis included:

- Reflection coefficient and operating frequency range
- Input impedance
- Radiation pattern and **HPBW**
- Antenna gain and directivity
- Transmitted and received power measurements
- Far-field measurements and comparison with theoretical results using the **Friis transmission equation**

## Exercise B — Microwave Transmission Line Measurements

Experimental measurement of microwave transmission line and waveguide characteristics using a **Slotted Line, Oscilloscope, Frequency Meter, and Microwave Generator**.

Main measurements and analysis included:

- Frequency and guided wavelength
- Standing Wave Ratio (**SWR**)
- Input impedance and reflection coefficient
- Identification of inductive, capacitive, and resistive loads
- Measurement of the relative dielectric constant of materials using waveguide measurements
- MATLAB calculations for the experimental results
